# Common questions from buyers

## "We already have a CRM and a data provider."

Fernway does not replace either. Teams keep their CRM as the record and their data provider for contact details. Fernway sits before the first email: it reads what the company says about itself and turns that into a checked reason to write. Most customers export the qualified list into their CRM once a week.

## "AI emails all sound the same."

Every sentence about the prospect in a Fernway draft carries the quote it came from, and a line whose quote cannot be found on the company's own pages is cut before the rep sees it. Reps edit about one draft in three before sending.

## "Where does our data go?"

The scoring runs on a model on your own machine. Only the drafting step can use a cloud model, and you can turn that off. Nothing is shared with other customers.

## "How long until it is useful?"

Most teams score their first list on the first day. Writing the ideal customer profile takes about an hour with the sales leader.
