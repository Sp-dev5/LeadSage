# Customer stories, part two

## Vaultmere: compliance software for mid-size banks

Vaultmere sells compliance monitoring software to mid-size banks and credit unions. Its six sales development reps were each researching about 25 banks a week by hand. After moving research into Fernway, each rep covered 70 banks a week, and the team booked 38 percent more first meetings in the next quarter.

## Fixloom: field service software for heating and cooling contractors

Fixloom sells scheduling and dispatch software to heating, ventilation and cooling contractors. Contractors rarely have a website that says much, so reps struggled to find a reason to write. Fernway picked up job posts for new service technicians as a sign of growth, and replies to first emails went from 4 percent to 9 percent.

## Learnmoor: training software for corporate learning teams

Learnmoor sells a learning platform to corporate training and HR teams at companies with more than 1,000 staff. Its account executives used Fernway to find companies hiring for learning and development roles, and cut the time to build a target list from two weeks to three days.

## Credvane: lending software for fintech companies

Credvane sells a lending API that fintech companies use to offer credit inside their own apps. The buyers are product and engineering leaders, which is a hard audience to write to. Fernway drafts that quoted the prospect's own product announcements doubled the reply rate, from 3 percent to 6 percent.

## Adsorrel: marketing analytics for online retailers

Adsorrel sells marketing analytics to online retailers and direct-to-consumer brands. The team used funding news as the reason to write: a brand that had just raised money was about to spend it on marketing. Five of its twelve largest new customers in the year came from first emails that opened on a funding round.
