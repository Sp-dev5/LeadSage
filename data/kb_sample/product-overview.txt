Fernway: product overview

Fernway is a research assistant for outbound sales teams. Before a rep writes to a company, Fernway reads the company's own website, its job board and its recent news, checks how well it fits the ideal customer profile, and drafts a first email that only says things the rep can check against a source.

Who buys it. Our buyer is the Head of Sales or VP of Sales at a B2B software company with 50 to 500 employees and an outbound team of at least three account executives or sales development reps. Revenue operations leads often run the trial.

The problem we solve. Reps at our customers spent two to three hours a day reading websites and writing first emails by hand. Most of that research never reached the email, and good moments to write, such as a new sales hire or a funding round, were noticed weeks late.

How it is priced. Fernway is priced per seat per month, billed yearly. A team of five reps is the smallest plan. There is no setup fee.

What it does not do. Fernway does not send email on its own. Every draft is approved by a rep and sent from the rep's own mailbox.
