Our rule

Every line about them comes with their own words behind it. No source, no sentence.

02 · Proof

Every line about them has a source.

Every sentence about the company carries a quote. LeadSage checks that the quote is in the company's own text. If it is not, the sentence is cut.

  • Exact quotes.The quote behind each sentence has to appear on their page word for word.
  • Names and numbers checked.A name, a place or a number in the sentence has to be in their text too.
  • Dated.Each line keeps the page it came from and the day it was read.

03 · Control

Nothing goes out without you.

A

You send every email

The app never sends mail. You approve each draft, send it from your own mailbox and mark it sent.

B

Scoring stays on your machine

Scoring and contact finding use a local model. Drafting can use a free cloud model or stay local.

C

Opt-out built in

Every email ends with an opt-out line, and a do-not-contact list keeps a company out, even after rescoring.
