How it works

From a list of companies to an email you can stand behind

Give it company names and websites. It does the reading, you make the calls.

Score

Fit, with reasons

Each company is read from its own website and scored against your ideal customer. Every score shows what it was based on.

Find

The right person

It reads the company's team and leadership pages for the person who runs sales. No guessed addresses, no LinkedIn scraping.

Draft

A reason to write now

The first email opens with something timely, like open sales roles or recent news, and every line about the company is checked against their site.

Follow up

Three times, then stop

Up to three follow-ups, 3, 7 and 14 days after the first email. A reply ends the sequence.

Proof

Every line about them has a source

Before a sentence about the company reaches the draft, LeadSage looks for the words it is based on in the company's own text. If they are not there, the sentence is cut.

  • Quoted, not paraphrased. The quote has to appear on their page as written.
  • Names and numbers checked. A place, a figure or a job title in the sentence has to be in their text too.
  • Dated. Each line keeps the page it came from and the day it was read.

Control

Nothing goes out without you

You send every email

The app never sends mail. You approve each draft, send it from your own mailbox and mark it sent.

Runs on your machine

Scoring and contact finding use a local model. Drafting can use a free cloud model or stay local.

Opt-out built in

Every email ends with an opt-out line, and a do-not-contact list keeps a company out, even after rescoring.
